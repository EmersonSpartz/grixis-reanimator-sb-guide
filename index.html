4Thoughtseize
Positive matchup pre- and post-board. Bauble counters their Mishra's Bauble, flashbacked Lava Dart, Surgical. If they play Mystical Dispute / blue bounce, bring in your own Disputes (shave 2 Emperor of Bones). If you show Pyroclasm G2 or they Surgical you, swap Pyroclasm for Dispute G3 on the play (keep Pyroclasm in on the draw). Every life point matters — minimize fetch loss.

vs. RW Land Destruction▼
JPA
IN
2Vexing Bauble
2Consign to Memory
OUT
4Fatal Push
Unpleasant matchup. Curve low enough that LD doesn't really hit until late game, but lots of spot removal + sweepers. Prefer them Eroding your creature over Path — Eroded creature can be reanimated. Mana Tithe threat is annoying / gets in your head. Their best SB card is Rest in Peace — keep Spell Pierce ready. If willing to hold 3 mana for Cancel, consider Dispute over Consign. Consign upside: counters Emperor of Bones' delayed sac trigger, potentially saves an Archon. Jam creatures and hope.
Final Thoughts (JPA)
Core cards never boarded out: Psychic Frog, Archon of Cruelty, Persist, Thought Scour. A T2 Archon/Frog is too powerful to mitigate in any matchup. Thought Scour fuels everything, is an instant (keeps up Consign/Push), and is the only card that lets you combo with nothing in hand/yard: mill Archon → draw Persist (3 mana), or Scour + Unearth Oculus (2 mana).
